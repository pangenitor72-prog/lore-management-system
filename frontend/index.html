What is this?

An AI-powered storytelling game. You pick a genre, describe a character, then tell the AI what you want to do. It's like a choose-your-own-adventure, but you can do anything.

How long should I try it?

Even 10-15 minutes is helpful! Play until you get stuck, confused, or bored — that's valuable feedback.

How do I give feedback?

Look for the pink feedback button in the corner. Click it anytime to tell me what's working or what's confusing!

There are no wrong answers. Just have fun and be honest!
