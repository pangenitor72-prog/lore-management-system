How to Play

Step 1 of 5

🎲 The Setup

Welcome to the tavern! You're playing against two cunning opponents in a game of bluffing and deception.

Each player starts with 5 dice. You can see your own dice, but your opponents' dice are hidden.

Your Goal
Be the last player with dice remaining!

💬 Making Bids

On your turn, you make a bid about how many dice of a certain face exist across ALL players' hands combined.

Example Bid
"I bid there are at least 4 Threes"

This means you're claiming that when all dice are revealed, there will be at least 4 dice showing the number 3.

📈 Raising the Stakes

Each bid must be higher than the previous one. You can raise by:

  • Increasing the quantity (e.g., 3 Fours to 4 Twos)
  • Increasing the face value with the same quantity (e.g., 3 Fours to 3 Fives)

The bidding continues around the table, with stakes getting higher each time.

⚠️ Calling the Bluff

If you think someone is lying, press Challenge! instead of bidding.

All dice are revealed and counted:

  • If the bid was true (enough dice exist) → You lose a die
  • If the bid was false (not enough dice) → They lose a die
Tip
Count your own dice of each value to estimate the total!

🎯 Controls & Strategy

Keyboard shortcuts:

  • Enter Place your bid
  • C Challenge the current bid

Know your opponents:

  • Gruff Bartender — Aggressive, tends to overbid
  • Hooded Stranger — Cautious, quick to challenge
