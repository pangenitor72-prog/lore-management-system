The Seer's Wager

Two seers sit across from each other, reading omens in the flight of birds. Each omen card you play reveals a vision - and a chance to claim the pot.

You and your opponent each start with 5 coins. First to claim all 10 wins the game.

Each round, you'll play 3 omen cards to form your vision. The seer with the strongest total claims the pot.

The Five Omens

🐷
Crow (2-5)
Thief - Steal 1 coin from opponent's bet
🦉
Owl (4-7)
Foresight - Draw a card, discard one
🦅
Hawk (6-9)
Strike - Opponent's omen loses 2 power
🦅
Vulture (3-6)
Scavenge - +1 power per card in discard
🐦
Raven (1-10)
Trickster - Copy the last ability played

How to Play

1. Ante Up - Each round starts with 1 coin from each player in the pot.

2. Play Cards - Click a card in your hand to play it. You and the opponent alternate, playing 3 cards each.

3. Abilities Trigger - When a card is played, its ability activates immediately.

4. Compare Omens - After 3 cards each, the higher total power wins the pot.

5. Raise - Before playing a card, you can raise the stakes by 1 coin. The opponent must match or fold.

Strategy Tips

Watch the discard pile - Vultures grow stronger as more cards are discarded.

Save your Ravens - The Trickster can copy powerful abilities at the perfect moment.

Raise with confidence - If you have strong cards, make your opponent pay to see them.

Hawks counter high cards - A well-timed Strike can swing the round.

The ancient Seer across from you is cunning but predictable. Learn their patterns!
