How to Play Skull

Skull is a game of pure bluff. No luck, no hidden information - just mind games.

You and your rival each have 4 tiles: 3 Flowers and 1 Skull.

Phase 1: Place Tiles

Take turns placing tiles face-down on your stack. You must place at least one tile before bidding.

Phase 2: Bid or Place

Instead of placing, declare: "I can flip X tiles safely!" Your rival can raise the bid or pass.

Phase 3: Reveal

The highest bidder must flip that many tiles. You must flip all your own tiles first, then choose from your rival's stack (top tile only).

  • Flip a Skull? You lose one of your tiles permanently (chosen randomly, hidden).
  • Succeed? You earn 1 point.
  • First to 2 points wins!

The trick: place your skull, then bid high. Make them flip it. Or bluff with all flowers and bid safe. Read your rival.
