How to Play

Shut the Box is a classic dice game of luck and arithmetic.

Goal: Flip down all 9 tiles, or leave the lowest sum possible.

Each Turn:
1. Roll the dice
2. Click tiles that sum to your roll
3. Confirm your selection
4. Repeat until no valid moves remain

Example: Roll a 7? You could flip down 7, or 1+6, or 2+5, or 3+4, or 1+2+4.

Scoring: Sum of remaining tiles. Lower is better. Shut all 9 = perfect score of 0!

Winning: You and the Barkeep each take a turn. Lowest score wins the pot.
