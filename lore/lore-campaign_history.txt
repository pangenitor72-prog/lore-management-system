Faction Type: Tech-worshiping scavenger collective with air superiority
IDEOLOGY & BELIEFS
Machine Spirit Worship & Tech-Hoodoo
The Vulture Clan believes machines contain spirits or souls. Technology is sacred, and salvage is a religious act of recovering these machine spirits from the ruins. Their spiritualism is pragmatic and technology-based: "if it works, the spirit is present."
This is tech-hoodoo - cargo cult mysticism blending ritual with practical engineering. NOT nature worship, NOT sky/earth mysticism, NOT abstract philosophy divorced from machinery.
Core Philosophy
Salvage as Sacred Duty: Recovering pre-war technology honors the machine spirits trapped in ruins
Zero-Waste Doctrine: Every component must be purposed; waste is heresy and moral failing
Practical Mysticism: Scrap-Shamans interpret machine behavior as spiritual communication, but results matter - if tech functions, spirits approve
Judgment of Waste: Other factions condemned for wasteful technology use (especially fire-worshiping chrome fanatics)
The Rust Creed
"Purpose everything. Waste nothing. The spirits remember."
LEADERSHIP STRUCTURE
Decentralized Command
The Vulture Clan has NO supreme leader. Warrior chieftains operate independently, competing for salvage territories while coordinating through shared machine-worship philosophy and mutual respect for Scrap-Shaman spiritual authority.
Warrior Chieftains
Independent territorial commanders who claim and defend salvage sites. Each commands their own crews, aircraft, and roosts. Coordination occurs through:
Periodic gatherings at major roosts (territory negotiation, salvage dispute arbitration)
Shared zero-waste doctrine (common ideology prevents internal fracture)
Scrap-Shaman mediation (tech-priests arbitrate conflicts through spiritual authority)
Mutual survival interest (external threats require cooperation)
Competition vs. Cooperation: Chieftains compete aggressively for prime salvage but unite against external factions threatening Clan territories or philosophy.

Scrap-Shamans (Tech-Priests)
Religious authorities who maintain, repair, and bless salvaged technology. They interpret machine spirits, enforce zero-waste doctrine, and provide spiritual legitimacy to chieftain claims.
Authority Sources:
Technical expertise (only Shamans can awaken dormant machine spirits)
Spiritual interpretation (determine which salvage is blessed, which is cursed)
Ritual enforcement (excommunicate waste-heretics, bless expeditions)
Dispute arbitration (neutral parties in chieftain territorial conflicts)
Ritual Tools:
Staffs: Circuit board crowns with electrical discharge at top, battery clusters at base, hanging diagnostic cables
Chest Rigs: Sensor arrays, diagnostic panels, sacred gauges (functional tools + ornamental relics)
Most elaborate mask/tube systems in Clan (multiple environmental filters for toxic salvage work)
The Spark Doctrine: When Shaman staff discharges electricity during ritual, machine spirit presence is confirmed. Pragmatically: staffs are battery-powered diagnostic tools. Spiritually: spirits manifest through electrical arc. Both are true.

CULTURE & PRACTICES
Mask Doctrine
Every member wears full-face covering at all times. Masks, helmets, goggles, rebreathers - no face is ever shown. Revealing flesh is heresy.
Dual Purpose:
Practical: Radiation storms, toxic salvage sites, chemical contamination, airborne hazards
Spiritual: Machine face superior to flesh. To show weakness of flesh insults machine spirits.
Enforcement: Removing mask in public = immediate exile or execution (depending on chieftain judgment and Scrap-Shaman spiritual reading).
Aesthetic Philosophy: Rust-Obsessed Patchwork
Everything the Vulture Clan builds appears cobbled from scrap - and it is. But through Scrap-Shaman expertise and machine spirit blessing, their technology functions despite appearances.
Design Principles:
Layered, asymmetrical armor (scavenged flight suits, sand-wrapped robes, canvas cloaks, military webbing, industrial padding)
Metal plates bolted to cloth/leather layers
Visible wiring, tubes, cables worn as ceremonial adornment
Rust, oxidation, pitting celebrated (not hidden)
Every component repurposed from pre-war salvage
Color Palette:
Rust red and scorched black (dominant)
Oxidized brass and tarnished silver
Bleachbone white and sun-faded yellow
Hazard orange accents
Materials:
Frayed canvas, cracked leather, pitted metal
Scorched plastic, corded wiring
Salvaged circuit boards, bone charms
Tech as Sacred Adornment
Wires, cables, hoses worn across chest/neck like ceremonial jewelry. Hanging components, rotating fans, loose coils treated as religious talismans. Circuit boards, gears, and machine fragments integrated into armor as spiritual symbols.
Totemic Builds: Some warriors create blade-to-blade welded weapons, over-fused circuits, or symbol-carved tools as personal machine spirit shrines.
Cargo Cult Pragmatism
Religious devotion blends seamlessly with practical engineering. Ritual maintenance is both genuine spiritual practice and ensures machines stay functional. The line between mysticism and mechanics is deliberately blurred.
Example: Scrap-Shaman blesses salvaged motor with incense and chanting while simultaneously cleaning corrosion, checking connections, and testing voltage. Both actions are sacred. Both are necessary.
Vehicle War-Shrines
Vehicles are not merely transport - they are mobile temples, execution platforms, pilgrimage engines. Each vehicle is a relic containing multiple machine spirits.
Modification Philosophy:
Aggressive, spiked, rust-ridden aesthetics
Layered scrap plating and jagged metal protrusions
Large rig wheels, scorched tires, visible axles and pistons
Airborne lift platforms, buzzard-themed paintwork
Multiple exhaust stacks, forked battering frames
Interiors treated as shrines: bones wired into dashboards, switches engraved with salvage tallies

TERRITORY & OPERATIONS
Roosts (Mobile/Semi-Permanent Bases)
"Roost" is the Clan's term for any major base or encampment. NOT a single headquarters - multiple roosts scattered across salvage territories, reflecting decentralized structure.
Roost Types:
Mobile Convoys: Linked vehicle trains moving between salvage sites
Hidden Fortresses: Camouflaged bases in ruins or natural terrain
Hybrid Camps: Semi-permanent structures with rapid evacuation capability
Roost Functions:
Aircraft maintenance facilities (gyrocopter repair, glider construction)
Scrap-Shaman workshops (ritual tech blessing, machine spirit appeasement)
Sacred machine storage (most valuable salvage stored as relics)
Chieftain coordination centers (periodic gatherings for negotiation)
Training grounds (Buzzard pilot drills, Mandrake exo-limb practice)
Security:
Constant air patrols (gyrocopters circling perimeter)
Hidden locations (change when discovered or salvage opportunities shift)
Scavenger scouts (early warning networks)
Aerial advantage prevents ground assault
Population Distribution:
~5,500 Vultures spread across multiple roosts (not concentrated in single location).

FACTION RELATIONSHIPS
ENEMIES (Active Hostility)
Smoker Legion:
ARCH-ENEMIES. Ideological opposites representing incompatible philosophies:
Smoker Legion: Chrome worship, fire obsession, burn everything for speed and glory
Vulture Clan: Rust reverence, preservation doctrine, repurpose everything as sacred duty
Conflict Basis:
Legion burns/wastes technology = ultimate heresy to Vultures. Their fire worship and chrome fetishism represents everything Clan condemns. Legion hoards fuel Vultures need but philosophical hatred runs deeper than resource competition.
Lead Corps:
Professional military force using (and destroying) technology without spiritual respect. Their gun-manufacturer worship focuses on consumption of weapons rather than preservation. Standardized equipment philosophy offends Clan's sacred uniqueness doctrine.
Also: Lead Corps allied with Smoker Legion (Vultures' arch-enemies).
Confederacy of New Austin:
Expansionist government threatening salvage territories. Military occupation tactics destroy ruins Clan intends to salvage. View Vultures as raiders rather than religious scavengers (disrespect for machine spirit philosophy).
Longshoremen:
Condemned for wasteful technology use and disrespect of machine spirits. Their rust-worship seems superficially similar but focuses on decay/death rather than preservation/rebirth. Drowned Devil submarine could be sacred relic but instead used as pirate tool (heresy).
ALLIES (Limited Cooperation)
The Coyotes:
Occasional work arrangements. Black market smugglers appreciate Clan's "no questions asked" salvage acquisition and aerial transport capability. Neutral relationship based on mutual profit, not ideology.
NEUTRAL (No Major Conflicts)
Most other factions avoided unless salvage territorial disputes arise. Vulture Clan prefers isolation - other factions waste technology, but not worth fighting unless they directly threaten salvage sites.
