# WORLD OVERVIEW

**Setting:** WAR-HEAD is a post-apocalyptic world set approximately 240-250 years after global nuclear war devastated civilization. The primary region is the Southwestern United States, now a harsh wasteland of irradiated deserts, ruined cities, and desperate survivors.

**Tone:** Gritty survival mixed with dark humor, retrofuturistic technology, and elements of the supernatural. Chrome and rust coexist. Pre-war tech is sacred. Water is life.

# LOCATIONS

## Continental Scale

### North America
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** The post-nuclear continent, largely uninhabitable in the northern reaches due to nuclear winter effects. The southwest remains marginally survivable due to existing desert adaptation.
- **Aliases:** The Continent, The Old World

### Southwestern United States
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** The primary setting of WAR-HEAD. Encompasses the former states of California, Nevada, Arizona, New Mexico, Texas, Colorado, and Oklahoma. A patchwork of faction territories, contested wastes, and rare oases of civilization.
- **Aliases:** The Southwest, The Wastes, The Frontier

---

## States/Regions

### California
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** The western coast, dominated by the ruins of Los Angeles and the irradiated farmlands of the Central Valley. The Sierra Nevada mountains form its eastern barrier. Contested between multiple factions.
- **Aliases:** The Golden State (ironic), West Coast

### Nevada
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Desert wasteland home to the ruins of Las Vegas. The Mojave Desert dominates. Water is exceptionally scarce. Home to various raider bands and the occasional trading post.
- **Aliases:** The Silver State

### Arizona
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Sonoran Desert territory. Phoenix ruins serve as a major scavenging site. The Colorado River (what remains) is fiercely contested. Extreme heat makes daytime travel deadly.
- **Aliases:** The Furnace State

### New Mexico
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Home to The Furnace (Permian Basin region), sacred mountains, and pre-war military installations. The Smoker Legion's primary territory. Rich in fuel but poor in water.
- **Aliases:** The Land of Enchantment (pre-war)

### Texas
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Massive territory spanning from the Gulf Coast to the Chihuahuan Desert. The Confederacy of New Austin controls much of the habitable regions. Oil derricks still pump in places.
- **Aliases:** The Lone Star, Big Country

### Colorado
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Mountain territory with the Rocky Mountains providing natural fortification. Pre-war military bunkers dot the landscape. Cooler temperatures but harsh winters.
- **Aliases:** The High Country

### Oklahoma
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Transition zone between the plains and the southwest. Tornado activity makes permanent settlement dangerous. The Ogallala Aquifer makes it strategically valuable.
- **Aliases:** The Panhandle

---

## Deserts

### Mojave Desert
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Harsh desert spanning Nevada and California. Las Vegas ruins sit at its heart. Extreme temperature swings between day and night. Relatively low radiation compared to other regions.
- **Aliases:** The Mojave, Death's Playground

### Sonoran Desert
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Arizona's dominant biome. Saguaro cactus forests (mutated variants) provide limited resources. Phoenix ruins are the primary landmark. Deadly heat kills the unprepared.
- **Aliases:** The Burning Lands

### Chihuahuan Desert
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** The largest desert, spanning New Mexico and Texas. The Furnace is located here. Rich in mineral resources but brutal conditions. Smoker Legion territory.
- **Aliases:** The Big Empty

---

## Mountain Ranges

### Rocky Mountains
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** The spine of the continent. Provides natural barriers, defensible positions, and water sources from snowmelt. Many pre-war bunkers and military installations hidden within.
- **Aliases:** The Rockies, The Spine

### Sierra Nevada
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** California's eastern mountain wall. Separates the coast from the interior deserts. Passes through the mountains are strategically vital and heavily contested.
- **Aliases:** The Sierra, Snow Mountains

### Sangre de Cristo Mountains
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Sacred mountains in New Mexico. Name means "Blood of Christ" - appropriate given the red-tinged sunsets caused by atmospheric particulates. Vulture Clan considers them spiritually significant.
- **Aliases:** Blood Mountains, The Cristo Range

---

## Urban Ruins

### Los Angeles Ruins
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Massive urban sprawl reduced to irradiated rubble. The Longshoremen control the port areas. Endless scavenging opportunities but equally endless dangers. Feral ghouls infest the metro tunnels.
- **Aliases:** The Angels, LA Wasteland, The Sprawl

### Las Vegas Ruins
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** The neon graveyard. Casinos stand as monuments to pre-war excess. Water shortage makes permanent habitation impossible. Raiders use it as a staging ground.
- **Aliases:** Sin City, The Strip, Neon Graveyard

### Phoenix Ruins
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Sun-blasted urban skeleton. Extreme heat makes daytime scavenging suicidal. Night operations only. Rich in pre-war tech but heavily picked over.
- **Aliases:** The Valley of the Sun (ironic), Ash City

### Tucson Ruins
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Smaller urban ruin south of Phoenix. University facilities contained valuable research materials. Davis-Monthan AFB nearby holds military salvage.
- **Aliases:** Old Pueblo

### Austin Ruins
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Heart of the Confederacy of New Austin. Partially rebuilt and functioning as their capital. The Colorado River provides water. Most intact major city in the region.
- **Aliases:** New Austin, The Capitol

---

## Strategic Locations

### The Furnace
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** The Artesia-Carlsbad-Hobbs triangle in southeastern New Mexico. The Permian Basin's oil and gas reserves make this the most strategically valuable territory in the wasteland. Smoker Legion controls it absolutely. Refineries belch black smoke constantly.
- **Aliases:** The Basin, Smoker Territory, Flame Lands

### Permian Basin
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Geological formation underlying The Furnace. Contains vast petroleum reserves that the Smoker Legion extracts and refines. The source of their power and their religion.
- **Aliases:** The Black Reservoir, Surtur's Blood

### Ogallala Aquifer
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Massive underground water reserve beneath the plains states. Access points are among the most valuable real estate in the wasteland. Multiple factions fight for control.
- **Aliases:** The Deep Water, The Aquifer

### Fort Bliss
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Pre-war military installation near El Paso. Massive vehicle depot and training facility. Partially stripped but still contains military hardware. Contested territory.
- **Aliases:** The Fort, Bliss Armory

### Los Alamos
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Pre-war nuclear research facility in New Mexico. Heavily irradiated but contains invaluable scientific equipment and data. Expedition target for those seeking advanced tech.
- **Aliases:** The Lab, Atom City

### Kirtland AFB
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Air Force base near Albuquerque. Nuclear weapons storage facility. Extremely dangerous radiation levels but the ultimate prize for any faction seeking WMDs.
- **Aliases:** Kirtland, The Nuke Farm

### Gulf Coast
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Texas coastline along the Gulf of Mexico. Hurricanes and flooding make it dangerous. The Longshoremen have interests here. Oil platforms offshore still contain fuel.
- **Aliases:** The Coast, Hurricane Alley

### Drowned Devil
- **Type:** Location
- **Description:** Underwater repository of pre-war vehicles, machinery, and technology. Location known only to the Longshoremen who salvage from it. Named for the massive equipment visible beneath the water.
- **Aliases:** The Devil, The Drowned Cache

# FACTIONS

## Major Factions

### Smoker Legion
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Fire-worshipping petroleum cult controlling The Furnace and the Permian Basin oil reserves. They venerate Surtur, the fire giant of Norse mythology, treating petroleum as his sacred blood. Organized in a rigid hierarchy with Infernicus as their god-king. Their zero-waste doctrine mandates that every drop of fuel serves a purpose. Napalm jetpacks, flamethrowers, and incendiary weapons define their arsenal. They brand their slaves and their tithes. Brutal, efficient, and utterly devoted to the eternal flame.
- **Aliases:** The Smokers, Fire Cult, Surtur's Children, The Legion

### Vulture Clan
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Nomadic scavenger-technicians organized into mobile Cadres. They practice Cargo-Cult Pragmatism - salvaging and maintaining pre-war technology without fully understanding its origins. Cybernetic augmentation is common but must be earned through demonstrated worth. They communicate through multiple channels simultaneously (speech, hand signs, radio clicks). Their naming convention reflects their life journey: birth names become titles like "the-Splice" or "-Null" based on their role and deeds. Chrome represents mastery; rust represents failure. They claim no territory but range across the entire Southwest.
- **Aliases:** The Vultures, Scav-Techs, Chrome-Keepers, The Clans

### Lead Corps
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Professional mercenary company that fills the role of hired military in the wasteland. They work for whoever pays - guarding caravans, providing muscle for settlements, or waging proxy wars for factions unwilling to commit their own forces. Strict professionalism distinguishes them from raiders. They honor contracts, maintain military discipline, and value their reputation above all. Members are former soldiers, security forces, or those who proved themselves in combat.
- **Aliases:** The Corps, Mercs, Lead Men, The Professionals

### Aqua-Mercatorum
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Water-merchant guild controlling major aquifer access points and water distribution throughout the wasteland. They maintain neutrality in factional conflicts because everyone needs water. Extremely wealthy and influential. They broker deals, finance expeditions, and sometimes engage in water-based coercion. Their caravans are sacrosanct - attacking an Aqua-Mercatorum shipment brings unified retaliation from all factions who depend on their services.
- **Aliases:** Water Merchants, The Guild, Aqua-Mercs, The Wet Ones

### Confederacy of New Austin
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Attempt at rebuilding legitimate government centered on Austin, Texas. They control the most intact urban center in the Southwest and aspire to pre-war civilization. A council governs them, with representatives from major settlements. They maintain a standing military, collect taxes, and provide services to citizens. Critics call them naive; supporters call them hope. Their territory encompasses central Texas along the Colorado River.
- **Aliases:** The Confederacy, New Austin, The Government, Austin-folk

### Snake Nation
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Loose confederation of tribes in the desert regions, named for their survival philosophy: be adaptable, strike quickly, retreat into the wastes. They reject both the rigid hierarchy of the Smoker Legion and the technological focus of the Vultures. Traditional knowledge and desert survival skills are their currency. They know water sources, safe passages, and hidden caches that others don't. Generally hostile to outsiders but will trade.
- **Aliases:** The Snakes, Desert Tribes, The Nation

### Longshoremen
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Salvage guild controlling the coastal ruins and port facilities, particularly around Los Angeles and the Gulf Coast. They specialize in underwater salvage from the Drowned Devil and other submerged sites. Their diving equipment and maritime knowledge make them unique. They operate massive cranes, maintain functional boats, and have access to pre-war naval technology. Insular and protective of their salvage sites.
- **Aliases:** The Shore, Dock Workers, Salvagers, The Long

### Bastion
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Fortified settlement philosophy rather than a single faction. Bastions are heavily defended communities that prioritize walls, guns, and isolation over trade or expansion. They trust no one outside their walls. Multiple independent Bastions exist throughout the wasteland, sometimes trading with each other, sometimes warring. They represent humanity's bunker mentality.
- **Aliases:** The Forts, Bunker-folk, The Walled

### Church of the Penitent Choir
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Religious order that views the nuclear apocalypse as divine punishment for humanity's sins. They preach penance, suffering, and redemption through service. Their "choir" refers to the collective voice of the faithful. They run hospitals, orphanages, and hospices throughout the wasteland, asking only for faith in return. Some see them as saints; others see them as manipulators building power through charity.
- **Aliases:** The Church, The Choir, Penitents, Holy Folk

## Minor Factions

### Warlords
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** General term for independent strongmen who control territory through personal force. Not organized into a faction per se, but a category. Each warlord is unique - some are brutal tyrants, others are benevolent dictators. They rise and fall constantly, their territories shifting with their fortunes.
- **Aliases:** Bosses, Lords, Strongmen

### Raiders
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Catch-all term for violent scavengers who take from others rather than produce. No organization beyond individual gangs. They attack caravans, settlements, and travelers. Most are desperate or cruel. Some graduate to more organized factions; most die violently.
- **Aliases:** Bandits, Gangers, Marauders

### Slavers
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Those who trade in human flesh. Not a unified faction but a profession. The Smoker Legion brands their slaves. Others simply collar them. Slavery is common in the wasteland - someone has to work the dangerous jobs. Some slavers specialize in capture; others in transport; others in sales.
- **Aliases:** Flesh-Traders, Collars, Slave-Takers

### Coyotes
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Human smugglers and guides who move people through dangerous territory for a price. Named for the pre-war term. They know the safe routes, the hidden paths, the patrol schedules. Some are honorable; many are not. Getting abandoned in the wastes after paying a coyote is common.
- **Aliases:** Guides, Smugglers, People-Movers

## Smoker Legion Subunits

### Legionnaires
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Basic infantry of the Smoker Legion. Every member starts here. They carry the fuel, guard the perimeter, and die in the front lines. Promotion requires proving devotion through deed. Standard equipment includes flamethrower sidearms and branded armor.
- **Aliases:** Grunts, Foot-Flames
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

### Pretorians
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Elite guard of Infernicus himself. Hand-picked from the most devoted and capable Legionnaires. They wear ornate armor decorated with flame motifs and carry the finest incendiary weapons. Fanatically loyal to the god-king.
- **Aliases:** The Guard, Flame Guard
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

### Warbosses
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Field commanders of the Smoker Legion. Each commands a warband and answers to the Five Pillars. Warbosses earn their position through combat and devotion. They have significant autonomy in their operations but absolute obedience to orders from above.
- **Aliases:** Bosses, War-Leaders
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

### Dragonflies
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Airborne assault troops equipped with napalm jetpacks. The terrifying elite of Smoker Legion offense. They drop from above, unleash fire, and withdraw. Extremely dangerous but their fuel consumption is enormous. Used for critical operations only.
- **Aliases:** Fly-Boys, Burners, Sky-Fire
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

### Combustiators
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Heavy weapons specialists carrying massive flamethrowers and incendiary launchers. They break fortifications and enemy formations. Walking arsenals of fire. Their equipment makes them slow but devastating.
- **Aliases:** Heavy Flames, Burners
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

### Black-Fingers
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Engineer caste responsible for maintaining refineries, vehicles, and equipment. Their hands are permanently stained with oil - hence the name. Technical expertise makes them valuable despite lower combat status.
- **Aliases:** Engineers, Grease-Monks
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

### Black-Hands
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Secret police and inquisitors of the Smoker Legion. They root out heresy, disloyalty, and waste. Everyone fears them. They answer only to Tithe-Brander Mammon and Infernicus himself. Black leather gloves are their badge of office.
- **Aliases:** Inquisitors, Secret Eyes
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

### Black-Tops
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Logistics and supply corps. They manage the fuel distribution, slave labor allocation, and resource tracking. Their ledgers determine who gets fuel and who goes without. Bureaucratic power in a flame-worshipping cult.
- **Aliases:** Quartermasters, Bean-Counters
- **Parent Faction:** Smoker Legion

## Vulture Clan Subunits

### Cadre
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Basic organizational unit of the Vulture Clan. A Cadre is a mobile group of 20-50 Vultures with their vehicles, salvage rigs, and equipment. Each Cadre has a Warchief, an Augur-Head, and various specialists. Cadres are named for their territory or notable achievement.
- **Aliases:** Crew, Band
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Buzzards
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Air scouts and light pilots of the Vulture Clan. They fly gyrocopters and light aircraft for reconnaissance, message-carrying, and rapid response. Named for their circling flight patterns while searching for salvage.
- **Aliases:** Fly-Boys, Scouts, Eyes
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Condors
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Heavy aircraft operators. While Buzzards fly light craft, Condors maintain and operate the rare functional cargo planes and bombers the Clan has salvaged. Extremely prestigious position.
- **Aliases:** Heavy-Wings, Big Birds
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Mandrakes
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Stealth operatives and infiltrators. Named for the poisonous plant - they're deadly but hidden. They scout defended positions, steal technology, and eliminate problems quietly. Minimal cybernetics to avoid detection.
- **Aliases:** Ghosts, Shadows
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Lappet Crews
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Salvage specialists who strip and process scrap. Named for the lappet-faced vulture known for stripping carcasses efficiently. They can disassemble anything and assess salvage value instantly.
- **Aliases:** Strippers, Scrap-Techs
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

## Specific Vulture Cadres

### Coilspring Basin Cadre
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Cadre operating in the spring-fed basin region. Known for water-finding expertise and maintaining positive relations with Aqua-Mercatorum. Their territory includes several hidden springs.
- **Aliases:** Springers, Basin Crew
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Blackite Highpass Cadre
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Mountain Cadre specializing in high-altitude operations and pass control. They know every route through the mountains and charge toll or trade for passage rights.
- **Aliases:** Highpass Crew, Mountain Vultures
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Ember Rig Cadre
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Cadre that operates in Smoker Legion border territory, specializing in trading with or scavenging from the fire cult. Dangerous work that requires diplomatic skill and quick escapes.
- **Aliases:** Ember Crew, Fire-Traders
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Jetstream Mountain Cadre
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Cadre with the highest concentration of aircraft. Their mountain base includes a functional airstrip. They provide air support to other Cadres in exchange for ground salvage shares.
- **Aliases:** Jet Crew, Air Cadre
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

### Sumpwell Basin Cadre
- **Type:** Faction
- **Description:** Cadre operating around a contaminated but resource-rich basin. They specialize in hazmat salvage and radiation-zone operations. Higher cybernetic replacement rate due to exposure.
- **Aliases:** Sump Crew, Rad-Runners
- **Parent Faction:** Vulture Clan

# CHARACTERS

## Smoker Legion Characters

### Infernicus
- **Type:** Character
- **Description:** God-king of the Smoker Legion. He is believed to be the mortal avatar of Surtur himself, speaking with divine authority. His body is extensively burned and scarred - marks of his communion with flame. He rarely appears publicly, ruling through his Five Pillars. When he does emerge, it's for holy pronouncements or to personally immolate enemies of the faith. His true age and origins are unknown, shrouded in religious mythology.
- **Aliases:** The God-King, Avatar of Surtur, Lord of Flames, The Eternal Ember
- **OCEAN Profile:**
  - Openness: 0.3 (Rigid religious doctrine, rejects outside ideas)
  - Conscientiousness: 0.9 (Absolutely methodical, every action is ritual)
  - Extraversion: 0.4 (Reclusive but commanding when present)
  - Agreeableness: 0.1 (Merciless, demands absolute obedience)
  - Neuroticism: 0.2 (Eerily calm, utterly certain of divine purpose)
- **Motivations:**
  1. Expand Surtur's dominion across all lands
  2. Achieve perfect zero-waste doctrine implementation
  3. Prepare for the Final Burning that will purify the world
- **Fears:**
  1. That his mortality will be revealed to the faithful
  2. Losing control of The Furnace's resources
  3. A schism within the Legion challenging his divinity
- **Secrets:**
  1. He is the third person to bear the title Infernicus - the role is inherited, not eternal
  2. He experiences moments of doubt about Surtur's existence
  3. He keeps a hidden water cache - heresy against his own zero-waste doctrine
- **Plot Threads:**
  1. Succession crisis: Who becomes the fourth Infernicus?
  2. Discovery of his human origins could shatter the Legion
  3. A Pillar plots to expose him and claim divinity themselves

### Crude-Lord Octaneion
- **Type:** Character
- **Description:** First of the Five Pillars, master of the refineries and fuel production. He controls the literal lifeblood of the Legion. Brilliant chemical engineer with an intuitive understanding of petroleum processing. His position makes him arguably more powerful than Infernicus in practical terms - without his refineries, the Legion has no fuel.
- **Aliases:** The First Pillar, Refinery-Master, Lord of Black Blood
- **OCEAN Profile:**
  - Openness: 0.6 (Genuinely curious about chemistry and processes)
  - Conscientiousness: 0.95 (Obsessive about efficiency and waste prevention)
  - Extraversion: 0.3 (Prefers his refineries to people)
  - Agreeableness: 0.4 (Practical rather than cruel, but unyielding on doctrine)
  - Neuroticism: 0.5 (Anxious about production quotas and equipment failure)
- **Motivations:**
  1. Perfect the refining process to extract every useful molecule
  2. Discover new petroleum reserves to ensure the Legion's future
  3. Gain recognition as the true power behind the throne
- **Fears:**
  1. The wells running dry
  2. A refinery disaster that kills his crews
  3. Being seen as merely a technician rather than a spiritual leader
- **Secrets:**
  1. He's identified a new oil field but hasn't reported it - keeping it as leverage
  2. He's been experimenting with non-petroleum fuel sources (absolute heresy)
  3. He despises Infernicus as a religious puppet with no technical understanding
- **Plot Threads:**
  1. His secret oil field could be discovered - or he could use it to stage a coup
  2. His heretical experiments could revolutionize the wasteland - or see him burned
  3. Growing tension with other Pillars over resource allocation

- **Type:** Character
- **Description:** Second Pillar, supreme military commander of all Legion forces. A tactical genius who has never lost a major engagement. Massive man covered in ritual burn scars, each marking a victory. He leads from the front, wielding a custom flamethrower called "Götterdämmerung." Absolutely loyal to the Legion but privately considers Infernicus a figurehead.
- **Aliases:** The Second Pillar, War-Master, The Burning Hand
- **OCEAN Profile:**
  - Openness: 0.2 (Military traditionalist, trusts proven tactics)
  - Conscientiousness: 0.85 (Strategic planning, logistics, discipline)
  - Extraversion: 0.7 (Charismatic battlefield commander)
  - Agreeableness: 0.2 (War is not about being nice)
  - Neuroticism: 0.3 (Calm under fire, occasional battle-rage)
- **Motivations:**
  1. Crush all enemies of the Legion absolutely
  2. Be remembered as the greatest military commander in wasteland history
  3. Expand Legion territory to the coasts
- **Fears:**
  1. Dying of old age rather than in battle
  2. A military defeat that stains his record
  3. The Legion growing soft without external enemies
- **Secrets:**
  1. He's planning a massive offensive against the Confederacy of New Austin
  2. He's been in secret communication with Lead Corps about hiring them
  3. His loyalty is to the Legion, not to Infernicus - he would support a capable replacement
- **Plot Threads:**
  1. His planned offensive could trigger a wasteland-wide war
  2. Alliance with Lead Corps could give him the power to challenge other Pillars
  3. What happens when he's too old to fight?

- **Description:** Third Pillar, high priest and propaganda master. He shapes the religious doctrine, writes the hymns, and crafts the mythology that binds the Legion together. Silver-tongued orator who can make burning alive sound like a blessing. His sermons are famous throughout the wasteland. True believer who genuinely thinks he speaks Surtur's will.

  1. Spread Surtur's word to every corner of the wasteland
  2. Create religious texts that will outlast the Legion itself
  3. Experience a true divine revelation
- **Fears:**
  1. That Surtur is not real and he's wasted his life on a lie
  2. Losing his voice (literally - it's his power)

  2. He's never actually felt divine presence despite claiming constant communion
  3. He's been writing a private journal questioning everything - if found, it's death
- **Plot Threads:**
  1. His journal could destroy the Legion's faith or spark reformation
  2. What if he actually experiences something supernatural?
  3. A rival preacher emerges with a different interpretation of Surtur

# CONCEPTS

### Surtur Worship
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Religious doctrine of the Smoker Legion. Surtur, the fire giant of Norse mythology, is venerated as the true god. Fire is sacred. Petroleum is his blood. Combustion is prayer. The Final Burning will purify the world of the unworthy.
- **Aliases:** Fire Faith, Surtur's Way, The Burning Path

### Zero-Waste Doctrine
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Core Smoker Legion philosophy. Every drop of fuel must serve a purpose. Waste is sin. Efficiency is virtue. This extends beyond fuel to all resources. Strict rationing, severe punishment for waste.
- **Aliases:** Anti-Waste, Efficiency Doctrine

### Chrome vs Rust
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Vulture Clan philosophy. Chrome represents mastery - maintained tech, working systems, earned augmentations. Rust represents failure - neglected equipment, degraded abilities, dishonor. The binary defines social standing.
- **Aliases:** Chrome Law, Rust Shame

### Cargo-Cult Pragmatism
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Vulture Clan approach to technology. They salvage and maintain pre-war tech without fully understanding its scientific principles. Function matters more than theory. If it works, it works. Religious overtones around "machine-spirits."
- **Aliases:** Tech-Faith, Machine-Spirit Doctrine

### Cybernetic Maintenance Philosophy
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Vulture Clan doctrine on augmentation. Cybernetics must be earned through demonstrated worth. They must be maintained constantly. Non-functioning augments are shameful - better to remove them than display rust.
- **Aliases:** Chrome Code, Augment Law

### Mask Boundary
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Vulture Clan belief that masks and face-coverings create a boundary between the self and the machine. Removing one's mask is intimate. Showing your face to strangers is taboo except in specific ritual circumstances.
- **Aliases:** Face Law, Mask Rule

### Vulture Naming Lifecycle
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Vulture Clan naming convention. Birth names are replaced with earned names reflecting role and deeds. Format: [Description]-[Role/Status]. Names change throughout life as status changes. Examples: Stillhand-the-Splice, Razorback-Null, Blackwell-Denied.
- **Aliases:** Name Earning, Chrome Naming

### Multi-Channel Communication
- **Type:** Concept
- **Description:** Vulture Clan practice of communicating through multiple methods simultaneously - speech, hand signals, radio clicks, light flashes. Allows layered messages and coded communication even when observed by outsiders.
- **Aliases:** Channel-Speak, Multi-Talk
