# AIRPG — Content Pressure Doctrine

## Purpose

This doctrine defines how authored content may enter the AIRPG runtime
without corrupting its foundational guarantees.

Content is treated as *pressure*, not truth.

This doctrine exists to prevent narrative authority, memory smuggling,
or canonical collapse via prose.

---

## Definition: Content

In AIRPG, **content** is any authored input that introduces:
- claims
- descriptions
- rumors
- reports
- tone
- framing

Content is **not**:
- canon
- fact
- memory
- state
- authority

Content is inert until processed by personality and topology.

---

## Core Principle

> **Authored content must behave like input, not outcome.**

Content may influence behavior.
Content may not decide behavior.

---

## Explicit Permissions (Content MAY)

Content may:
- Introduce claims (e.g., “The duke is dead.”)
- Introduce ambiguity or uncertainty
- Be emotionally or stylistically framed
- Be contradictory to other content
- Be transformed during propagation
- Be rejected or halted by NPC personality
- Be re-injected identically across runs

---

## Explicit Prohibitions (Content MUST NOT)

Content must never:
- Persist across interactions implicitly
- Be stored as world memory
- Resolve conflicts globally
- Override personality outcomes
- Encode authority (“this is true”, “it is known”)
- Force propagation or termination
- Carry hidden state or identifiers
- Assume prior injections
- Accumulate belief outside the engine’s logic

Any violation constitutes **content corruption**.

---

## Determinism Requirement

For identical:
- content
- topology
- personality configuration

The engine must produce identical traces.

Content cannot introduce randomness, time dependence,
or cross-run variance.

---

## Conflict Handling Doctrine

When multiple content units conflict:
- The engine does NOT resolve truth
- No canonical winner exists
- NPC personality governs reaction
- Conflicts may coexist indefinitely

Truth is not collapsed by content.

---

## Tone Separation Doctrine

Tone may affect:
- phrasing
- emotional framing
- stylistic transformation

Tone may NOT affect:
- propagation reach
- topology
- belief resolution logic
- memory behavior

Style is never state.

---

## Runtime Boundary

Content exists only at runtime.

When the interaction ends, content is gone.

Any persistence must be explicit, external,
and orchestrator-driven — never implicit.

---

## Enforcement Requirement

No content feature may be merged unless:
- A sanity check exists proving non-corruption
- Conflicting content has been tested
- Determinism has been verified
- No memory leaks are observed

Doctrine is enforced in code, not belief.

---

## Final Warning

Narrative is the most dangerous vector for cheating.

If content ever “feels right” but violates this doctrine,
the content is wrong.

AIRPG prefers correctness over comfort.
