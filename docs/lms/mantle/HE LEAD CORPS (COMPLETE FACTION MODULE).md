HE LEAD CORPS (COMPLETE FACTION MODULE)
================================================================================

OVERVIEW
--------
Size: ~9,000-10,000
Territory: Southern California fortress-cities (reclaimed military bases, arms manufacturing plants, munitions depots)
Leader: Bullet-Lord, Supreme War-General Harland Barrett
Structure: Military hierarchy with manufacturer-titled officers, enlisted ranks use birth names
Ideology: Military supremacy, weaponry obsession, "might makes right through firepower"
Military: Professional army, best-armed faction in wasteland, best-equipped with advanced weaponry, armored vehicles, rare tanks
Allies: Smoker Legion, Chain-Gang (subordinate auxiliary)
Enemies: Confederacy, Snake Nation, Vulture Clan, Church

SUMMARY
-------
Militarized raider empire descended from West Coast military remnants (active-duty soldiers, National Guard, first responders). Started as protectors after the Fall, devolved over generations into authoritarian warlords, then full raider-slaver faction. Retain professional military structure and discipline despite brutal practices. Control pre-war arms manufacturing plants and military depots, making them the best-equipped faction in the wasteland. Officers abandon personal names for firearm manufacturer titles, reinforcing their obsession with weaponry and war.

EVOLUTION TIMELINE
------------------
Year 0-20: Protectors/defenders of survivors
Year 20-60: Authoritarian enforcers ("martial law for your own good")
Year 60-120: Strong-arming, extortion, protection rackets
Year 120-present: Full military warlord cult - all pretenses dropped
Current Status: So far detached from original protective mission it's unrecognizable. Rank-and-file are true believers in "order through strength" ideology. Slavery justified through layered propaganda: "prisoners of war," "forced labor for reconstruction," and openly "they lost, we won."

LEADERSHIP & STRUCTURE
----------------------

SUPREME LEADERSHIP
------------------
Bullet-Lord, Supreme War-General Harland Barrett
- Absolute ruler of the Lead Corps
- Birth name: Harland; "Barrett" applied as Supreme Commander title (Barrett Firearms reference)
- Ruthless iron-fisted despot, quick to anger, intolerant of failure
- Immortan Joe meets Bullet Farmer energy - believes his own twisted ideology while enjoying power/brutality
- Personally oversees military campaigns, arms production, slave-driven war economy
- Holds total authority; officers fear and revere him equally

OFFICER NAMING SYSTEM
---------------------
Officers abandon personal names for firearm manufacturer titles. The title belongs to the rank/position, not the individual.
Cultural Significance:
- Titles are earned through displays of brutality, efficiency, unwavering loyalty
- More prestigious manufacturer = higher status (Brigadier Kalashnikov > Lieutenant Walther)
- Losing rank = losing title (disgraced officers renamed to lesser titles or executed)
- Title stays with position: If Captain Remington dies, successor becomes Captain Remington
- Only ONE officer holds each title at a time (no duplicate Captain Remingtons)

Rank Tiers & Manufacturer Categories:
- Brigadiers: Heavy weapons and artillery manufacturers (Kalashnikov, Browning, Rheinmetall)
- Captains: Infantry firearm manufacturers (Luger, Remington, Koch, Heckler)
- Governors: Shotgun and hunting rifle manufacturers (Benelli, Winchester, Mossberg)
- Lieutenants: Precision firearm and special weapons manufacturers (Colt, Steyr, Barrett, Walther)

HIGH COMMAND OFFICERS (Named NPCs)
-----------------------------------
BRIGADIERS (Regional Commanders):
- Brigadier Kalashnikov - Oversees siege warfare and armored battalions
- Brigadier Browning - Commands heavy weapons divisions and artillery forces
- Twin Brigadiers Smith & Wesson - High-ranking female officers
  * Control military intelligence and elite counterintelligence operations
  * Elite enforcement units and internal purges (secret police function)
  * Work nearly directly under Barrett
  * Infamous for cold, methodical approach to war, executions, purges
  * Aesthetic: Overly capable, strict officer energy (feared even by other officers)

GOVERNORS (Territorial Rulers):
- Governor Benelli - Controls enslaved settlements and tribute extraction
- Governor Winchester - Oversees munitions factories and arms trade operations

CAPTAINS (Company Leaders):
- Captain Luger - Specializes in urban raids and police-style enforcement
- Captain Remington - Leads frontline infantry and tactical strike teams
- Captain Koch & Captain Heckler - Brothers who coordinate mechanized assaults and vehicular warfare (Heckler & Koch split into partnership)

OTHER RANKS:
- Taskmasters & Overseers: Manage slave labor operations, war production, population control (rank + name, manufacturer titles where appropriate)
- Enlisted Ranks: Soldiers and enforcers use rank + birth names only (Private James, Sergeant Rodriguez, Corporal Martinez, etc.)

TERRITORY & STRONGHOLDS
-----------------------
Primary Territory: Southern California fortress-cities
Stronghold Types:
- Reclaimed military bases (pre-war installations fortified and expanded)
- Arms manufacturing plants (pre-war factories restored to production via slave labor)
- Munitions depots (strongest fortifications - fuel their military superiority)
- Vehicle depots (maintain rare pre-war military vehicles)

Foreign Presence:
- New Angel (Los Angeles): Heavily defended weapons exchange depot / embassy in Snake Nation territory (trade munitions/armor for slaves/fuel/spoils from Southern Gate district)

MILITARY CAPABILITIES
---------------------

FIREARMS & EQUIPMENT
--------------------
Standard-Issue Weapons:
- Modified pre-war military rifles (M16s, M4 Carbines, HK G3s, FN FALs)
- Pistols & sidearms (Beretta M9s, SIG Sauer P226s, Glock 17s)
- Shotguns (Mossberg 500s, Benelli M4s) for close-quarters combat
- Light machine guns (M249 SAWs, RPKs) for suppressive fire
- Heavy weapons (M2 Browning .50 cal MGs, Mk 19 grenade launchers, AT4 rocket launchers)
- Sniper & DMR weapons (M14 EBRs, Barrett .50 cals, Dragunovs)

Specialized Weaponry:
- Limited prototype/experimental firearms scavenged from pre-war research sites
- Rare energy weapons (extremely limited, salvaged)
- Custom-modified officer weapons (engraved stocks, extended magazines, personal modifications)

Armor & Attire:
- Reinforced military surplus gear (plate carriers, tactical vests, riot gear)
- Scrap-modified Kevlar & ballistic armor with metal plating
- Officer distinctions: Customized trench coats, sashes, heavily reinforced armor with rank insignia
- Soldier adornments: Spent brass casings, bullet chains (symbolizing dedication to war)

VEHICLES & WAR MACHINES
-----------------------
Lead Corps is a SPECIAL CASE - best vehicle access in the wasteland due to controlling pre-war military depots and manufacturing plants.
Light Vehicles:
- Up-armored technicals (trucks/jeeps with mounted machine guns or recoilless rifles)
- Dirt bikes & ATVs (scouting, flanking maneuvers)

Heavy Vehicles:
- Modified Humvees & MRAPs (heavily reinforced for convoy escort and urban warfare)
- War-rigs & armored transports (repurposed cargo trucks with plated armor, turrets, slave cages)
- Scavenged APCs (M113 APCs, LAV-25s - operational via slave labor maintenance)
- Salvaged pre-war tanks (extremely limited numbers, kept operational through slave labor, rare deployment)

Siege & Artillery:
- Mortar emplacements & artillery cannons (bombarding enemy settlements)
- Heavy weapons emplacements at fortress-cities

Air Assets:
- Rarely operational helicopters & VTOLs (stored at select locations, only used in critical assaults)

TACTICS
-------
- Shock-and-awe raiding: Overwhelming force against smaller settlements
- Forced recruitment: Captives choose - join or die
- Enslavement and occupation: Captured territories converted to slave-driven production centers
- Convoy warfare: Heavily armed mobile columns (direct attacks = suicidal)
- Siege warfare: Artillery and armored assaults on fortified positions
- Urban enforcement: Police-style raids and occupation tactics

ECONOMY & RESOURCES
-------------------
Primary Industries:
- Slave trade & forced labor - Captured survivors produce weapons, fuel, resources
- Arms manufacturing - Slaves work in weapons factories creating crude but effective war materials (also maintain/restore pre-war equipment)
- Raiding & extortion - Settlements pay tribute or face destruction
- War economy - Entire faction built on continuous warfare and expansion

Trade:
- Weapons/ammunition/armor exchange with other factions (especially Smoker Legion, presence in New Angel)
- Slave sales (some to Chain-Gang for resale, some kept for internal labor)
- Scrap metal and salvage from conquered territories

CULTURE & PRACTICES
-------------------

CORE BELIEFS
------------
- Survival through dominance - The strong rule, the weak serve
- Military supremacy - Professional discipline + raider brutality
- Weaponry obsession - Guns represent power, heritage, identity (secular fanaticism, NOT spiritual worship)
- Might makes right through firepower - Superior weapons = legitimate authority
- "Order through strength. Wealth through war." (faction motto)

MILITARY CULTURE
----------------
- Strict hierarchy and discipline (unlike chaotic raider bands)
- Officer prestige based on manufacturer title earned
- Constant competition for rank advancement (through displays of brutality/efficiency)
- Veterans of major campaigns respected across ranks
- Military traditions retained (salutes, formations, battlefield protocol) despite moral corruption

SLAVERY SYSTEM
--------------
Justification layers (depending on audience):
- "Prisoners of war" (enemy combatants)
- "Forced labor for reconstruction" (society needs workers)
- "They lost, we won" (might makes right - open cynicism)

Slave Operations:
- Lead Corps brands slaves with property identification marks (different from Chain-Gang torture/ID system)
- Slaves work in arms factories, vehicle maintenance, construction, mining
- Mortality rate high; constant need for fresh captives fuels raiding
- Some slaves forcibly recruited as cannon fodder troops (fight for Corps or die in chains)

EXECUTIONS
----------
- Military efficiency style: Firing squads (not spectacle like Church)
- Public executions used for discipline and intimidation
- Quick, professional, brutal

FACTION ICONOGRAPHY
-------------------

VISUAL STYLE
------------
Simple utilitarian military markings - white spray paint or stencils (NOT elaborate custom artwork)

Common symbols:
- Skull with crossed rifles (primary symbol)
- Skull with bullets for teeth
- Skull (side view) with grenade pin at top
- Crossed bullets
- Crossed rifles
- Simple skull + weapon combinations
- Unit variations (Artillery Brigade markers, company insignias)

Applied to:
- Vehicles (stenciled on doors, hoods, armor plating)
- Armor (painted on chest plates, helmets, shoulders)
- Banners and flags
- Fortress walls

Aesthetic distinction: Lead Corps keeps it functional and intimidating, not artistic. Quick military stencils, not elaborate paint jobs.

RELATIONSHIPS
-------------

ALLIES
------
Smoker Legion
- Trade partners and occasional military coordination
- Both benefit from each other's resources - Lead Corps weapons for Legion fuel access

Chain-Gang (Subordinate auxiliary force)
- Once independent slaver faction, now pledged service to Lead Corps
- Semi-autonomous operations (manage own slave raids and black market)
- Boundaries and tribute expectations enforced by Lead Corps
- Used as cannon fodder, enforcers, dirty work specialists
- Separate entity but clearly subordinate

ENEMIES
-------
Confederacy of New Austin
- Territorial rivals, ideological opposition (Confederacy claims legitimate government authority)

Snake Nation
- Raiding conflicts, territory disputes (despite trade relationship in New Angel)

Vulture Clan
- Tech scavenging conflicts (Lead Corps sees them as scavenger trash, Vultures see Corps as wasteful)

Church of the Penitent Choir
- Ideological friction (Church sees Corps as godless military thugs, Corps sees Church as delusional zealots)

NEUTRAL/COMPLEX
---------------
Aqua-Mercatorum
- Occasional trade (water for weapons) but mutual distrust

Bastion
- No direct relationship; Bastion sees Lead Corps as irredeemable savages

CAMPAIGN HOOKS & PLOT THREADS
------------------------------

POTENTIAL STORYLINES
--------------------
1. SLAVE REVOLT
   Players encounter enslaved settlement planning uprising. Do they help? Risk Lead Corps retaliation?
2. OFFICER ASSASSINATION
   Lead Corps officer (Captain, Governor, Brigadier) targeted for removal. Players hired as assassins or defenders?
3. ARMS DEAL GONE WRONG
   Trade with Lead Corps turns violent. Players caught in middle of factional conflict.
4. FORTRESS SIEGE
   Lead Corps besieging player-allied settlement. Players must break siege or negotiate surrender.
5. DEFECTION OPPORTUNITY
   High-ranking officer wants to defect, needs extraction. Information they possess could cripple Lead Corps operations.
6. MANUFACTURER TITLE RIVALRY
   Two officers compete for same title after predecessor dies. Players manipulated as pawns in internal power struggle.
7. TWIN BRIGADIERS' PURGE
   Smith & Wesson conducting loyalty purge. Players falsely accused, must clear names or flee.
8. VEHICLE HEIST
   Lead Corps convoy transporting rare tank/APC. Players plan heist for reward or faction ally.
9. SLAVE BRAND REMOVAL
   Players need to remove Lead Corps brand from escaped slave (medical/technical challenge).
10. BARRETT'S WRATH
    Supreme War-General personally leads campaign. Players face his forces or flee region entirely.

GM NOTES
--------

RUNNING LEAD CORPS ENCOUNTERS
-----------------------------
Combat Style:
- Professional, coordinated, disciplined
- Use military tactics: suppression fire, flanking, retreat/regroup
- Officers call plays, enlisted follow orders
- Never mindless charges - always strategic

Negotiation:
- Officers respect strength, competence, military bearing
- Civilians/raiders treated with contempt
- Former military PCs get +2 to Diplomacy/Intimidate with Lead Corps
- Weakness = death sentence (negotiate from position of strength or don't negotiate)

Moral Complexity:
- Rank-and-file genuinely believe "order through strength" ideology
- Some officers are pragmatic (can be reasoned with)
- Others are true believers (fanatics, no compromise)
- Slaves may be victims OR former raiders who deserve punishment (player judgment call)

Weaknesses to Exploit:
- Rigid hierarchy (kill officer, soldiers may falter)
- Fuel-dependent vehicles (sabotage supply lines)
- Slave labor (inspire revolts, disrupt production)
- Internal rivalries (pit officers against each other)

DESIGN NOTES
------------

THEMATIC CORE
-------------
Lead Corps represents "law and order" taken to fascist extreme. They ARE organized, disciplined, effective - but those traits serve brutal oppression. Military professionalism without morality. Weaponry as identity without humanity.

KEY TENSION
-----------
Players may respect their efficiency while hating their methods. Some PCs might even JOIN Lead Corps (military background, pragmatic survival, seduced by "order"). GMs should allow this but show consequences.

AVOID
-----
- Making them cartoonishly evil (they have internal logic)
- Treating slavery as joke/background flavor (it's horrific, show impact)
- Ignoring their competence (they're GOOD at war, that's why they're dangerous)

EMPHASIZE
---------
- Professional brutality (they file paperwork after massacres)
- Manufacturer worship as cultural identity (guns = gods = self)
- Evolution from protectors to predators (tragedy of slow corruption)
- Contrast with other raiders (disciplined vs. chaotic, structured vs. anarchic)
