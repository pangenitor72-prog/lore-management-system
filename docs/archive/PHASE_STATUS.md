The following capabilities were proven without reliance on memory, time, randomness,databases, or campaign state:
- Deterministic expression of OCEAN personality traits through behavior and dialogue
- Parameterized personality variation using identical scene structure
- Multi-NPC interaction with emergent dominance, interruption, and concession
- Inversion of power dynamics purely through OCEAN profile changes
- Clean separation between personality logic and future systems (memory, time, factions)
These proofs establish personality as a first-class engine input suitable forintegration with future systems such as memory, decoherence, faction logic, andbelief propagation.
No further work is required in Phase 1.

## Phase 4C — Information Saturation / Termination (COMPLETED)
Phase 4C validated that information spread deterministically terminates or saturates based purely on topology and personality-gated forwarding.

The following capabilities were proven:
- Propagation naturally terminates if no personalities choose to forward information.
- Propagation saturates all reachable nodes if personalities are permissive.
- Information spread rate and extent are controlled by a combination of network topology and NPC personality traits.
- The propagation process is fully deterministic, yielding the same step-by-step delivery map for identical inputs.
- No memory, time, randomness, or state is involved in the propagation decision-making.

This establishes a critical mechanism for controlling the reach and lifespan of information within AIRpg, ensuring that rumors and news do not automatically "flood the world" but are instead shaped by the agents and their connections.

Phase 4C is complete.

## Phase 6C — Belief Dominance (COMPLETED)
Phase 6C validated that repeated belief pressure can deterministically tip outcomes toward dominance, without introducing memory, counters, decay, weights, time, or persistence.

The following capabilities were proven:
- Belief category convergence: The belief category stabilizes after repeated pressure application.
- Stability without memory: The convergence occurs despite each run being stateless, with no stored history.
- Dominance without accumulation: Stability emerges from consistent external pressure (injected prior belief label), not internal data accumulation.

This proves that belief behavior can appear cumulative even when the system has no memory of prior runs, using only present-time inputs.

Phase 6C is complete.
