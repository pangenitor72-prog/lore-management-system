Insight: Small LLMs Are Programmable Systems

(Captured: 2025-11-13)

I realized that small language models (3B, 7B, 8B) don’t behave like flagship models (GPT-5, Claude, Gemini).
They don’t respond to vibe, nuance, or conversational prompting.
Instead:

Small models must be programmed, not prompted.

They require:

clear rules

deterministic steps

strict schemas

explicit constraints

reduced ambiguity

short, precise instructions

state-machine logic

This fits the way my brain works.
This is the mindset shift that will shape how I build the LMS, MANTLE, and the Abominable Orchestrator.
It’s the bridge between coding and prompting.

Long-term goal:

Learn to program small models using:

deterministic scaffolds

rule-based prompting

multi-model orchestration

control-flow prompts

specialized agents

This is a permanent part of my development philosophy now.
