# World Logic Charter
**Version:** 1.2  
**Last Updated:** November 6, 2025  
**Status:** Conceptual Design (Future Phase II)

---

## Overview

The **World Logic Charter** is a universal set of narrative coherence laws that apply to ALL fictional worlds in the LMS. These laws govern physics, causality, and narrative consistency independent of genre or setting.

**Purpose:**
- Provide baseline "physics" for fictional worlds
- Detect narrative contradictions automatically
- Guide contradiction reconciliation
- Enable portability across genres

**Integration:** Future Phase II (after Phase I core functionality)

---

## The Eleven Laws

### LAW I: Conservation of Consequence
**Statement:** "Every action must have a traceable cause and a proportional effect."

**Meaning:**
- Nothing happens without reason
- Effects match causes in scale
- Butterfly effects must be explained
- No deus ex machina without setup

**Violations:**
- Random events with no cause
- Disproportionate effects
- Plot conveniences without justification

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "The kingdom fell because the heir was assassinated."
❌ INVALID: "The kingdom fell for no reason."
```

---

### LAW II: Limited Exception
**Statement:** "Every wonder must carry a cost, a conduit, or a consequence."

**Meaning:**
- Magic requires price/source/aftermath
- Power isn't free
- Shortcuts have drawbacks
- Balance must be maintained

**Violations:**
- Unlimited power with no cost
- Magic with no explanation
- Consequences ignored

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "Casting the spell drained his life force."
❌ INVALID: "He cast unlimited spells with no cost."
```

---

### LAW III: Local Truth (Flexible Exceptions)
**Statement:** "Exceptions to other laws are permitted IF they are consistently applied within a defined scope."

**Meaning:**
- Local rules can override universal ones
- But must be consistent within scope
- Sacred grounds, dead zones, special locations
- "Here, magic works differently"

**Scope Examples:**
- Inside the Sanctum, magic is costless (overrides Law II)
- In the Dead Zone, magic fails entirely
- Within the city walls, time flows differently

**Requirements:**
- Clearly defined boundaries
- Consistent application
- Explained to reader/players

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "Inside the temple, the dead can speak (local exception)"
❌ INVALID: "Sometimes the dead speak, sometimes not (inconsistent)"
```

---

### LAW IV: Reconcilable Conflict
**Statement:** "Contradictions must be resolvable through narrative means."

**Meaning:**
- Conflicting facts can coexist with explanation
- Unreliable narrators are valid
- Perspective differences are acceptable
- Mystery ≠ contradiction

**Resolution Methods:**
- Unreliable narrator
- Different perspectives
- Time period changes
- Lost/corrupted information
- Deliberate misdirection

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "He claims to be immortal, but witnesses saw him die. (unreliable narrator)"
❌ INVALID: "He's immortal. He died. (unexplained contradiction)"
```

---

### LAW V: Persistent Identity
**Statement:** "An entity cannot be and not-be simultaneously without explanation."

**Meaning:**
- Entities have consistent existence
- Death/transformation must be explained
- Retcons require narrative justification
- "Schrödinger's NPC" is invalid

**Violations:**
- Character alive and dead with no explanation
- Entity exists in contradictory states
- Unexplained resurrections

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "He died, then was resurrected by dark magic."
❌ INVALID: "He's both alive and dead."
```

---

### LAW VI: Temporal Ordering
**Statement:** "Events must maintain chronological consistency or provide a framework for paradox."

**Meaning:**
- Cause before effect (usually)
- Timeline must be trackable
- Time travel requires rules
- Flashbacks must be identified

**Violations:**
- Effect before cause (without time travel)
- Contradictory timelines
- Inconsistent chronology

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "The prophecy warned of the war. Then the war happened."
❌ INVALID: "The war happened. The prophecy warned of it later."
```

---

### LAW VII: Bounded Knowledge
**Statement:** "Characters can only know what they could plausibly have learned."

**Meaning:**
- No omniscience without explanation
- Information requires source
- Secrets require discovery
- Knowledge must be earned

**Violations:**
- Characters knowing impossible facts
- Information from nowhere
- Unexplained omniscience

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "He learned the secret from the ancient tome."
❌ INVALID: "He just knew the secret somehow."
```

---

### LAW VIII: Material Permanence
**Statement:** "Physical objects cannot spontaneously materialize or vanish without a mechanism."

**Meaning:**
- Conservation of mass (mostly)
- Items require origin
- Destruction requires cause
- Teleportation needs explanation

**Violations:**
- Items appearing from nowhere
- Things vanishing without cause
- Unexplained creation/destruction

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "The sword was forged by the dwarves."
❌ INVALID: "The sword just appeared."
```

---

### LAW IX: Proportional Power
**Statement:** "An entity's capabilities must be consistent with its nature and history."

**Meaning:**
- Power levels must be justified
- Growth requires time/training
- Abilities match background
- No sudden power-ups without explanation

**Violations:**
- Instant mastery
- Inconsistent power levels
- Unexplained abilities

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "After years of training, he mastered the technique."
❌ INVALID: "He suddenly knew how to fight perfectly."
```

---

### LAW X: Geographical Coherence
**Statement:** "Locations must maintain spatial relationships and traversal costs."

**Meaning:**
- Distance matters
- Travel takes time
- Geography is consistent
- Maps should work

**Violations:**
- Inconsistent distances
- Impossible travel times
- Contradictory geography

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "The journey from A to B took three days."
❌ INVALID: "A to B is both 100 miles and 10 miles away."
```

---

### LAW XI: Social Consistency
**Statement:** "Cultures and societies must operate by internally consistent rules."

**Meaning:**
- Social structures make sense
- Customs are followed
- Economy functions
- Politics are logical

**Violations:**
- Contradictory social rules
- Impossible economies
- Illogical politics

**Example:**
```
✅ VALID: "The matriarchy is ruled by women, as tradition demands."
❌ INVALID: "The matriarchy is ruled by men."
```

## How the Charter Works

### Three-Tier System

```
TIER 1: Universal Charter (This Document)
↓ Applies to ALL worlds
    
TIER 2: Campaign Setting Document  
↓ World-specific overrides
    
TIER 3: Local Truth (Law III)
↓ Scene-specific exceptions
```

### Validation Process

```python
def validate_entity(entity):
    # Check against Charter Laws
    charter_violations = check_charter(entity)
    
    # Check against Campaign overrides
    campaign_violations = check_campaign_rules(entity)
    
    # Check for Local Truth exceptions
    local_exceptions = check_local_truths(entity)
    
    # Return violations with reconciliation guidance
    return violations, guidance
```

## Campaign Overrides

Each campaign can override Charter laws for world-specific rules:

```yaml
# campaign_overrides.yaml
charter_overrides:
  - law_number: 2
    law_title: "Limited Exception"
    override_type: "refinement"
    campaign_statement: "In Hollow Eye, magic costs SANITY not life force"
```

See `concepts/campaign-overrides-format.md` for full specification.

## Contradiction Reconciliation

When contradictions are detected, the Charter provides guidance:

### Law IV Reconciliation Methods:
1. **Unreliable Narrator:** "The character was mistaken"
2. **Perspective Difference:** "From their view, it seemed..."
3. **Time Period:** "This was true then, changed later"
4. **Lost Information:** "Records were corrupted"
5. **Deliberate Deception:** "They were lying"

### Reconciliation Workflow:
```
1. Detect contradiction
2. Identify which laws are violated
3. Check for Local Truth exceptions (Law III)
4. Suggest reconciliation per Law IV
5. DM approves/modifies reconciliation
6. Store narrative resolution
```

## Example: Charter in Action

### Scenario: Character Death Contradiction

**Contradiction Detected:**
- Document A: "Sir Aldric died in battle at Helm's Gate"
- Document B: "Sir Aldric attended the feast last week"

**Charter Analysis:**
- **Violates:** Law V (Persistent Identity) - can't be dead and alive
- **Violates:** Law VI (Temporal Ordering) - if dead first, can't attend later

**Reconciliation Options (Law IV):**
1. **Perspective:** "Reports of his death were false (mistaken identity)"
2. **Temporal:** "The feast was BEFORE the battle (documents out of order)"
3. **Supernatural:** "He was resurrected between events (Law II - magic cost)"
4. **Unreliable:** "The witness lied about seeing him at feast"

**DM Chooses:** Option 2 (feast before battle)

**System Records:**
```json
{
  "contradiction_id": "...",
  "charter_guidance": "Violates Laws V & VI",
  "reconciliation_type": "temporal_reordering",
  "reconciliation_narrative": "The feast occurred before Sir Aldric's death at Helm's Gate. Documents were presented out of chronological order."
}
```
